How to read this
#
The pair's number.
What the user does
The thing a visitor tries on your site.
Device
We test as a phone and as a laptop.
How we check
We pick the simplest way to check each test, and open a real browser only when a test needs a click or a look. Each check has a tag showing which way was used.
Code
Reading the page. The code reads its text and decides (does it open, which words show, how many results).
AI
Reading the page. The AI answers a yes/no question about its text.
Real browser
Used when a test needs a click or a look. It clicks through like a shopper (never pays or logs in). Then the code counts the cart, or the AI looks at a screenshot.
You check
The last resort, always with a reason: a payment, login or "are you a robot?" page, the AI wasn't sure, or the real browser wasn't available. You answer Looks right or Looks wrong.
Result
Pass, Fail or Can't test (the site blocked us, or it waits for your answer).
What the site showed
A short piece of what the site sent back.
Why
A one-line reason for each fail, or for why a check was handed to you.
