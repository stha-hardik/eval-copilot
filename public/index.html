Example · from a longer made-up list

3 things to fix first

No long reports. We put the problems that matter most on top, with a fix in plain words.

  1. Test 2Phone

    One tap adds two lamps

    Site showed: The cart showed 2 lamps after one tap.

    Plain-words fixMake the Add to cart button ignore a second tap until the first one is done.

  2. Test 7Phone

    The checkout button is covered

    Site showed: A cookie banner sits on top of the Checkout button.

    Plain-words fixLeave some space at the bottom of the page so the banner never covers the button.

  3. Test 3Laptop

    The cart is behind a robot check

    Site showed: A page asking “are you a robot?” instead of the cart.

    Plain-words fixLet our test visitor past the robot check on a test copy of your site.

How it works

Four steps, no code

Describe what users do in everyday words. We do the testing and tell you what to fix.

  1. Step 1

    Add your site

    Give us the link to your website. That's all we need to get going.

    yourshop.example
  2. Step 2

    Write your tests

    Say what a user does and what should happen, in your own words. We can suggest some too.

    Adds one lamp to the cart once
  3. Step 3

    Run them on phone and laptop

    Every test runs on your live site. We use the simplest check, and a real browser only when a test needs a click or a look.

    PassFailCan't test
  4. Step 4

    Fix, re-run and see it pass

    Fix what broke, then run just those tests again and watch them turn green.

    FailFixed
